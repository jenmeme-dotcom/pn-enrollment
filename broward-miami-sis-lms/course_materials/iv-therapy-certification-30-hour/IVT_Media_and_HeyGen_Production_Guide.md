# BMHI IV Therapy Media and HeyGen Production Guide

## Recommended production approach

Use the instructor scripts embedded in the speaker notes of every PowerPoint slide. Import each module into HeyGen, select one professional nursing instructor avatar, and paste the matching slide script into the scene narration. Use captions and a calm instructional pace. Keep demonstrations clearly labeled as simulation or task-trainer practice.

## Lowest-cost media sources

1. **Pexels first:** Free photos and videos for commercial use. Attribution is not required, but record the creator and source URL for BMHI's production file. https://www.pexels.com/license/
2. **Pixabay second:** Free stock photos and videos under the Pixabay Content License. Keep the original asset URL and download record. https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/
3. **Adobe Stock when clinical specificity matters:** Use licensed Adobe Stock assets when free libraries do not show the correct device, procedure, PPE, or clinical setting. https://stock.adobe.com/
4. **HeyGen for the instructor:** The free plan is appropriate only for short trials. For full course production, use a paid plan that supports PowerPoint import, longer videos, watermark removal, and the required output resolution. https://www.heygen.com/pricing

Do not use an image merely because it looks medical. A qualified nurse should review every selected asset for correct equipment, PPE, insertion technique, tubing setup, labeling, and patient positioning before publication.

## Production and licensing record

For every selected asset, retain the provider, contributor, asset title or ID, source URL, download date, license type, and the module/slide where it appears. Do not publish watermarked previews. Do not imply that a depicted person or organization endorses BMHI. Avoid visible patient identifiers, brand marks, medication labels, or unsafe technique.
