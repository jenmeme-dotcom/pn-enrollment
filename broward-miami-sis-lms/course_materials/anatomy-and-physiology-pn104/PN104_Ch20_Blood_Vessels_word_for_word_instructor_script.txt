SLIDE 21
Let us review the major takeaways from Chapter 20. Arteries carry blood from the heart, veins return it, and capillaries are where exchange occurs. Blood pressure is highest in the arteries and drives flow through the circuit. Capillary exchange delivers nutrients and removes wastes; imbalance causes edema. The systemic and pulmonary circuits carry blood to the body and the lungs. Fetal circulation bypasses the lungs through shunts that close at birth. This final slide connects Chapter 20 to practical nursing. Hypertension. Chronically high blood pressure damages vessels and organs. Atherosclerosis. Arterial plaque narrows vessels and raises clot risk. Pulse & BP. Core vital signs reflecting cardiovascular status. Edema. Fluid leaking from capillaries causes tissue swelling. Varicose Veins. Failed venous valves let blood pool in the legs. IV Access. Superficial veins are used for fluids, medications, and blood draws.
