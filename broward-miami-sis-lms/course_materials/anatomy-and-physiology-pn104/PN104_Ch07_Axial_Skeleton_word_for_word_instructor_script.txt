SLIDE 21
Let us review the major takeaways from Chapter 7. The skeleton divides into the axial (central axis) and appendicular (limbs) divisions. The skull's 22 bones protect the brain and form the face, joined by immovable sutures. The vertebral column supports the body, protects the spinal cord, and has four curvatures. The thoracic cage protects the heart and lungs and assists with breathing. The infant skull has fontanelles that allow birth and brain growth.
