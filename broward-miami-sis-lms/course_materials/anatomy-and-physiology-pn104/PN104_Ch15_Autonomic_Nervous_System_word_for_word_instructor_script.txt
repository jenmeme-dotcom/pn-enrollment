SLIDE 4
Now let's focus on The Sympathetic Division. The sympathetic division. The "fight-or-flight" system. Prepares the body for action. Raises heart rate and breathing. Arises from the thoracolumbar cord. Connect sympathetic activation with increased heart rate, dilated airways and pupils, reduced digestive activity, and the body's response to stress or danger.
